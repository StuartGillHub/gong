# Gong Pool

A still, dark pool that ripples with every gong in the music. Made for projecting on a wall.

- Each gong strike appears somewhere new on the water: a faint glow gathers there, as if a gong rested just beneath the surface, and a few fine rings of light drift slowly outward, easing to a near stop as they fade.
- While a gong keeps ringing, it sends out a single faint ring now and then, and its glow lingers.
- Different gongs are recognised by their sound and each has its own colour, from pale amber for the deepest through blush and lilac to pale teal and moonlight for the highest. Deep gongs tend to sound nearer the middle; higher ones wander further out.

## Sound sources

- **Audius**: search the open [Audius](https://audius.co) music platform for gong baths and similar music (no account or key needed). Tracks play one after another.
- **File**: play or drag in your own audio files.
- **Microphone**: listen to whatever is playing in the room.
- **Demo**: synthesised gongs, for trying it out with no music.

## Controls

`Space` play/pause · `F` full screen · `N` next track · `D` demo · `H` hide controls.
The controls and cursor fade away when the mouse is still. The settings let you adjust sensitivity, how readily a new gong is recognised, brightness, and detail (lower it if the picture stutters).

## Running it

It's a static page with no build step: open `index.html`, or serve the folder with GitHub Pages or any web server. Audius needs the page to be served from a normal web address (it won't load inside some embedded previews).
